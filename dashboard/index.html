How the yield is built

The model uses only the snapshot’s own figures. Every kind multiple is the median market cap to TVL of protocols in the same kind whose token already trades; thin samples are capped, never trusted upward.

Expected yield = TVL × kind multiple × vigor × years in field × distribution

Vigor

1 + 0.6 × the seven-day TVL change, clamped to −50% and +100%.

Years in field

0.82 + 0.18 × min(age in days, 1,460) / 1,460. Older protocols earn a patience premium; 0.90 when the snapshot has no start date.

Distribution

1 + 0.02 × min(chains − 1, 4). A protocol on more chains has more surface for a token.

Kind multiple

The median mcap/TVL of live protocols in the kind. Each ratio is held between 0.02× and 2× before the median, so one outlier cannot set a kind. With fewer than four live comparables, the lower of the kind’s median and its wider group’s; with none, a stated fallback.
